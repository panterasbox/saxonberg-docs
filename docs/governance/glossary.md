# Glossary — the Compact's names

The names the meta-institution uses for itself, decided 2026-09-18 and
applied universally: in the constitution, the subsystem docs, the
requirements and plans, `OFFICE_APPARATUS`, the `/compact/*` rows and
every message a player reads. **One name per thing; the fiction is free
to use whatever the Compact does not.**

## The rule the names follow

The executive is a control loop: the engine *measures*, rules *act*
wherever the law is mechanical (Art. V §6), and humans sit in the loop at
exactly two points — **writing the code** and **exercising the judgment
code cannot** (§9). So:

- **The machine gets no titles.** A rule is a rule (*the window*, *the
  perpetual*, *the ladder*). The test for whether a thing is a
  mechanism or a person: *could a member read the Schedule and predict
  it?* Yes → a mechanism, named for what it does. No → a person did it,
  and the record says who.
- **The humans in the loop get familiar, prestigious titles** — the
  ministerial family, which reads from a fantasy realm to a modern state
  and whose head the constitution already names. Prestige is
  proportional to judgment: the only thing the machine cannot do is the
  only thing worth a title.
- **The markedness rule survives for the NOUNS, not the titles.** *The
  Compact, member, committee, the Houses* stay the meta's marked words.
  Titles are borrowed plain from civics, so the fiction's governments
  avoid them: a player who asks for *the minister* gets one answer.

## The names

| thing | name | notes |
|---|---|---|
| the meta-institution | **the Compact** | locked 2026-07-30 |
| the layer | **the meta** | never bare "Meta" |
| an enfranchised human | **a member** | never "citizen" (the fiction's word) |
| the group holding title over a subdivision | **a committee** | derived from title; players only; governance, never a job |
| the legislature | **the Houses** — the Producer House, the Capital House, the Consumer House | each with a **Speaker** |
| the executive's head | **the Prime Minister** | installed by confidence, removed by constructive no-confidence |
| the humans who hold the judgment over an institution | **Ministers**; collectively **the Cabinet** | not *secretary* (modern-only; bare, it reads as staff) |
| the body of institutions | **the Administration** | Art. V §9's word — "the executive administers" |
| an institution | named for what it does, with the definite article | never *board*, *bureau*, *authority* for the Compact's own |
| the central bank | **the Central Bank**; **the Reserve** colloquially | its head is **the Governor** — the cybernetic word (Watt's governor; *cybernetics* from the Greek steersman, the root of *govern*) |
| the fisc | **the Treasury** | **the Minister of Finance**; the city's charter keeps its *Treasurer* |
| goods, materials, recipes, tools — the physics of trade; the commons | **the Ministry of Trade** | **the Minister of Trade**; holds `/trade` and `/stuff` |
| corporations — their committees, charters, renewal | **the Registrar of Corporations** | the office and the officer share the name (Companies House's head); distinct from the fiction's *Registry*, a records office |
| the people who program the machine | **the Ministry of Works** | **the Minister of Works**; Art. V §6 — *programming the machine is an executive function*. Today the wizard axis with the Prime Minister as archwizard; a seat when a build needs one |
| the publishing organ | **the Press** | positions publish; no minister |
| the record | **the Archive** | Art. VII; an *Archivist* when one is needed |
| the judiciary | **the Court**; its members **Justices** | Art. VI |
| a single-holder seat in code | **an Office** (`OFFICE_APPARATUS`) | the apparatus word; a *position* is a job on a chart; a *seat* is the fiction's pointed-at position |
| staff | **positions**; *clerks* | a job, never governance |
| the founding role | **the Founder** | Art. XI; holds every Office by default until handed off |
| the levers | **the Schedule** (of Parameters) | the rows the rules read |
| making a character | **embody** (`embody <field> <value>`, `embody confirm`) — the first embodiment, `reembody` being the shade's; the moment is **arrival** | never *enroll* — enrolling is what you do at the University; the two happen minutes apart and are not the same act |
| the newcomer's note | **the Arrival Note** | a member's note to the Treasury, written by the machine at `embody confirm`, filed in their papers |

**Governor is a fine word wherever a seat steers a mechanism** — the
Reserve is the first; it is not reserved to it.

## What the fiction keeps

Uncontested and free for content: *government, state, law, court,
resident, citizen, council, mayor, magistrate, administrator, treasurer,
chancellor, registry, steward, reeve, sheriff, warden, keeper, master,
department, secretary, board*. A realm that wants *ministries* names
them a *council* instead, so *the minister* means one thing in chat.

## Retired by this glossary

*Treasurer* (meta) → the Minister of Finance · *Board of Trade* → the
Ministry of Trade · *Governor of the Central Bank* stays, spoken as
*the Governor* · the marked-register candidates considered and declined
(*Steward, Warden, Keeper, the Standards, the Register, the Bench*) —
too cute; the executive's names should be what we hope its officers
are, not a badge either way.
